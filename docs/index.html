How this site works
A transparent, evidence-first pipeline — not an AI-written news summary.
  1. Collects AI-in-health developments each day from ~60 curated sources — regulators, HTA & payer bodies, clinical journals, trial registries and industry press — through their official APIs and RSS feeds.
  2. De-duplicates by canonical link, so the same story from several sources appears once.
  3. Classifies every item into one of six stages — research, clinical evidence, HEOR & HTA, regulation, reimbursement, industry — using transparent keyword and source rules (no model).
  4. Scores & ranks items by explicit, attributable rules — new device authorisations, trials with an economic endpoint, actions from major regulators, and recency. Every item can show why it ranks where it does.
  5. Rebuilds the entire site automatically each morning via a scheduled job. No database, no server, no tracking.
  6. Never invents events or dates. Dates are read from the source; when none is present the item reads “date unknown” rather than a guess. No causal claims are generated beyond what the counts support.
