How it works
A transparent, evidence-first pipeline — not an AI-written news summary. Built for researchers, clinicians, investors, regulators and market-access teams tracking AI adoption in healthcare.
1

~65 curated sources — regulators, HTA & payer bodies, journals, trial registries, industry publications — via official APIs and RSS.

↓
4

Explicit signals — device authorisations, economic-endpoint trials, major-regulator actions, recency. Ranking reflects priority, not certainty or confidence.
↓

Publish
Rebuilt automatically every morning. Static, privacy-preserving architecture: no user tracking and no personal data storage.
Editorial principles
  • Never invents events or dates. Dates are read from the source; when none exists the item reads “date unknown”, never a guess.
  • No causal claims. We report what changed and how unusual it is — never why, beyond what the counts support.
  • Primary sources preferred. Official machine-readable feeds and APIs where they exist; carefully scoped news queries only where none does.
  • Company press releases excluded, to keep the feed independent.
  • Ranking is priority, not confidence. Order follows explicit additive rules, and every item shows its own “Why ranked” breakdown.
Coverage & cadence
Coverage philosophy
We prioritise primary regulators, HTA agencies, trial registries, peer-reviewed literature and established trade publications. Native feeds where they exist; scoped news queries where they don’t — a deliberate editorial choice, not a technical limitation.
Cadence
Rebuilt once each morning. Most items are a day or two old; device authorisations reflect the FDA’s ~30-day publishing lag.
